About

Full-stack, top to bottom

I'm a full-stack web and mobile developer with a strong foundation across both ends of the stack — React.js, React Native, HTML5, CSS3 and JavaScript on the interface side, and PHP, Laravel, Python, Flask and Node.js underneath. I specialize in scalable, high-performance e-commerce platforms built from scratch, and I've taken products from a blank repo to the App Store, Google Play and Huawei AppGallery.

Clean architecture and user-centred design guide everything I build: responsive, secure, SEO-friendly applications with APIs that actually hold up in production. GraphQL for dynamic data, Docker for containerized deployment, SQLAlchemy and raw SQL for the parts that need precision — I get it done, and I get it shipped.

I've worked with UK startups, product teams, designers and international freelance clients, turning business requirements into digital products people actually use.

Currently building at Plannit Limited (UK) — shipping web and mobile features to production every sprint.
